## Other observations

- **niri's event order around a workspace switch.** Switching to an empty workspace, niri reported the workspace as focused before it reported focus leaving the old workspace's window; switching back, before it focused the workspace's window. An earlier version ended `focus_workspace`'s wait at the workspace event and reported the old window, or none, as focused. The check now also waits for keyboard focus to reach the workspace's active window, or no window on an empty workspace.
- **Review findings fixed in this milestone.** A focus on the already-focused workspace would let niri's `workspace-auto-back-and-forth` switch away while the tool reported `focused`; nothing is sent for a target that already has focus. A single-instance app answering `launch` by focusing the window it had was reported as `interrupted`; it is `focused` with that window. A stop during the evidence screenshot threw the observed outcome away; the screenshot now runs after the stop race.
- **Protocol tests.** Every outcome and refusal is also checked over stdio against the in-process fake niri: `uncertain` with `accepted: null` when niri holds the reply past two seconds, a stop cancelling a running action and taking the lease back, `screen_locked` after the lock state changes while the lease is held, and a failed capture leaving the outcome with `screenshot_error`.
